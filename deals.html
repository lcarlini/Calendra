Feed throttled? Add a free rss2json key (optional)

The feed is read through rss2json.com, which limits free use per address. A free key from rss2json.com removes the limit. It is stored only in this browser and sent only to rss2json.com. The News page uses the same key.

Prices shown on a thread are only what the title or first lines say, and are not checked. Community activity does not mean an offer is genuine or still valid. Only the headline, a short excerpt and a link are shown; open the thread to read it. The feed lists the newest threads only (older pages are not available).

Find coupons

Looks for coupon codes that people post in the community feed, and gives you ready searches on coupon sites. This page only finds: nothing is registered or stored, and Calendra cannot tell whether a code still works.
