Price & Deals

One community deal feed is read live. Stores and price-comparison sites have no permitted automated access, so for those this page gives you a ready search and lets you track prices yourself. Nothing here is a verified price: numbers are either labelled with their source or typed by you.

Search

Community feed: Adrenaline Ofertas

Prices shown on a thread are only what the title or first lines say, and are not checked. Community activity does not mean an offer is genuine or still valid. Only the headline, a short excerpt and a link are shown; open the thread to read it. The feed lists the newest threads only (older pages are not available).

Products I'm tracking

Add a product and record the prices you see. History, trend and alerts use only the prices you enter (there is no automatic price checking, because no store offers a permitted way to do it).

Coupons

Coupons you find are saved by hand. They stay marked unverified: Calendra cannot tell whether a code still works.

Alerts

Alerts appear here when a price you record meets a rule you set. Browser notifications only work while this page is open. Email, push and webhooks are not available: they would need a server and an email service.
