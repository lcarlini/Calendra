Markets

Data comes from public central-bank and exchange-data APIs that a browser can read. Quotes are delayed, end-of-day values are labelled as such, and anything that cannot be loaded is shown as unavailable instead of estimated.

Policy interest rates

Policy targets (Selic, Fed funds range, ECB deposit rate) are set by committees. The effective federal funds rate is a market outcome and is shown separately. Rates change a few times a year, so they are not polled every second.

Currencies

Daily closing rates from the Central Bank of Brazil (sell rate). These are end-of-day values, not a live feed.

Brazilian stocks (B3)

Default list: a curated example of large, well-known B3 tickers. It is not a live ranking by market capitalization or volume and not a recommendation. Edit it freely.

US stocks, ETFs and indices

Automated quotes unavailable. The free US market-data sources tested (Yahoo Finance, Stooq) block browser requests or rate-limit them, and no US provider with browser access is configured, so no US prices are shown and none are estimated.
