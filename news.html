News

Headlines from the outlets' own public RSS feeds, grouped by story. The headline, a short excerpt and the picture the publisher puts in its feed (loaded from the publisher, credited, tap to enlarge) link to the original. Stories covered by more outlets rank higher, which says nothing about whether they are true.

Sources

Each source is integrated through its own public RSS feed or marked unavailable. Links open the publisher.

    How stories are grouped and ranked

    • Duplicates: the same article through different links, or the same headline inside one publisher group, is merged.
    • Clusters: different articles about the same event are grouped when they share enough distinctive words, in the same language and within 48 hours. Different languages are never merged.
    • Coverage: UOL and UOL Economia count as one outlet, as do g1 and Globo.com. Identical headlines at different outlets (syndicated copies) and repeated updates count once.
    • Ranking: breadth of coverage first, then freshness (in 6-hour steps), then position in the outlets' feeds. There is no numeric score.
